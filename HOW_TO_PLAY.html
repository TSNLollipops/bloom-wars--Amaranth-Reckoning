SEC. 01

Controls

Everything happens by clicking the board. There's no drag, no hotkeys, no right-click menu.

Click a unit
Selects it. The board lights up around it: green tiles it can move to, red enemies it can hit from where it's standing, cyan allies a Munti can Repair instead of attacking.
Click green
Moves there. Costs 1 action, does not end your turn — the unit stays selected and its options recompute, so you can move again or still act.
Click red
Attacks. Costs every action the unit has left and ends its turn immediately, no matter how many actions were still in the bank.
Click cyan
Munti only — heals that ally instead of attacking. Costs 1 action, does not end the turn (so a Munti with two actions free can heal two different allies in one turn).
End Turn
Bottom-right button. Resolves the hostile AI's whole turn, then the environment step (acid tiles, deploy-pad healing, shield/regen ticks), then hands the turn back to you.
< mission select
Top-right, bails out to the mission list at any time — nothing is saved mid-mission, so this is a hard restart of the current fight.
Campaign tabs
The mission-select screen has two tabs: Team One (the original 4-mission engine test) and Amaranth Act I (this manual). They're independent — different roster, different maps, same rules.
SEC. 02

Reading the board

No sprites yet — every unit is a shape. Shape says class, fill colour says side, outline says chassis.

Triangle — Meeps

Melee brawler. Dodges 40% of any hit it takes (house rule).

Square — Tank

Melee, high HP, projects a shield aura onto anyone standing next to it.

Diamond — Reeps

Ranged (2–4 tiles), never counterattacked. Gets +1 range standing on a ridge.

Circle with bar — Munti

Support. Weak in a fight; keeps everyone else alive instead.

Blue fill

One of yours — Warden Company.

Tan fill

A hostile mech. The game never names these — that's deliberate, not a bug.

Coloured blob

A Bloom creature — a plain circle, faded/translucent while burrowed.

Thick white outline = centauroid chassis (can Charge). Thin outline = bipedal/vibrissal. Dimmed to ~half-opacity = that unit has already used its actions this turn.

Vitalitythe real HP underneath

How Collapse actually works (verified from the ruleset, not flavour text)

A Bloom creature's Endurance (top bar) soaks damage first, and overflow does not spill into Vitality — a single huge hit against high Endurance is partly wasted, so finishing blows beat overkill. While Endurance is still above zero, the creature also hits softer as its shell wears down — its own outgoing damage scales down with its remaining Endurance fraction.

Once Endurance hits zero, it enters Collapse (a pulsing red ring appears around it): any single hit worth at least its remaining Vitality kills it outright — but its own attacks now land at full power, not weakened. A dying Bloom creature is not a safe one. Finish collapsed targets fast.

The class triangle

Meeps > Reeps > Tank > Meeps. Munti sits outside the triangle entirely — it's not built to win fights.

The one rule that makes this triangle work: a Reeps firing from range never eats a counterattack. That's the entire reason Reeps beats Tank despite the Tank's raw durability — keep your Reeps at range 2–4 and it's a one-way exchange.

SEC. 06

Abilities & house rules

A few of these aren't in the original design docs — added during Maxime's own playtesting. All of them are live in this build.

Overshield Tank, passive

While an Overshield Tank is alive on the board, every adjacent ally gets +1 terrain defence star (10% less damage taken). Doesn't stack with a second Tank.

Repair Munti, active

Instead of attacking: heal one adjacent ally 30 HP (38 if the Munti's mek runs Fieldwright as primary). Costs 1 action, doesn't end the turn.

Charge Centauroid, passive

Move 3+ tiles in an unbroken straight line over cost-1 terrain, then attack at the end of it → +25% damage.

Sensor Sweep Vibrissal, passive

On the sheet for vibrissal-chassis pilots (Cpl. Anand). Honest flag: burrow-reveal isn't wired up in this build yet — treat it as flavour for now, not a mechanical edge against Undertow.

Meeps Dodge house rule

40% chance to take zero damage from any single hit — as the target, and again on the counter-hit a Meeps eats after attacking something that counters back. Two independent rolls.

Tank Shield house rule

Overshield also grants a real 20-point shield (absorbs before HP) to the Tank and every adjacent ally. Regens 8/turn only if that unit took zero damage since the last tick. Step outside the radius and shield drops to 0 immediately — it's borrowed, not owned.

Munti Regen house rule

Every living Munti passively heals itself and same-side allies within 2 tiles for 8 HP/turn — free, stacks with active Repair, doesn't stack across multiple Muntis.

2 Actions / Turn house rule

Move and Repair each cost 1 action and don't end your turn — chain two moves, two Repairs on different allies, or move-then-Repair. Attack always burns every remaining action, whichever slot it's used in.

SEC. 07

Objectives

Three objective types across these four missions. Only one of them still has a hard clock.

eliminate_all

Kill every hostile. No turn limit — the turn count shown is a future bonus-scoring target, not a deadline. Only losing every one of your units ends the mission early.

hold_zone

Get a unit onto the gold hold tiles and keep every hostile off them from the hold-turn on. Real deadline — and if hostiles control the zone unopposed past turn 2, that's an instant loss.

extract_unit

Get the named unit onto a green exit tile before the turn limit. Real deadline — a deliberate rescue-mission clock, kept on purpose.

SEC. 09

Mission briefings — Act I

The four Amaranth missions currently in the build, with one tactical note each.

I.1 — Muster

eliminate_all6 Crawlmassbonus by turn 8

First light on the Fallow Line. A tutorial fight, meant to be an easy clean win.

Tip: no clock to fail on — take your time, keep the squad together, let Bosk lead the way in.

I.2 — Wire and Mud

hold_zone6 Splitfang, staggeredhold from turn 6, limit 10

Hold the forward listening post until the survey detail clears.

Tip: there is exactly one doorway into the hold room. Park Bosk in it — everyone else can spread out on the gold tiles behind him.

I.3 — The Low Ground

eliminate_all8 Crawlmass + 2 Splitfangbonus by turn 12

Bloom mat came up through the terraces overnight — a supply detail was caught crossing at first light.

Tip: the purple ground is bloom mat — 5 damage every turn you end a move on it (Sec. 03). Don't linger; kill and keep moving.

I.4 — Tunnel Rats

eliminate_all3 burrowed Undertow + 4 Crawlmassbonus by turn 12

First burrower contact on the Line.

Tip: burrowed units render faded but are already targetable at range — lead with Anand's Reeps (range 2–4, no counter) rather than walking a melee unit in blind.
