Part of the Qiraki files. Short in-universe excerpts for use as chapter openers, curriculum lines, poster copy, assembly speech fragments, official record language. Drop in with zero connecting commentary, let the chapter that follows do the contradicting on its own. Frequency, LOCKED, updated from an earlier "every several chapters" rule: every single chapter now opens with one, a deliberate escalation, not the original "texture, not gimmick" caution. **Exception, confirmed by author 2026-08-14: B1.CH01 is exempt.** Ch.1 opens cold, straight into Trav waking up, no epigraph. Decided, not an oversight, the arrival chapter earns its own opening rather than sharing it with an institutional fragment. Variety of source type carries the weight that frequency used to guard against, never two chapters running the same kind of document in a row. ## The Bloom curriculum, LOCKED, one bank per academy year

Delivered via the existing devices, propaganda-fragment chapter openers and study-session chapters, not a new mechanism. Three fragments per year, meant to be used a few per book rather than all at once. The throughline across all seven: Coalition curriculum keeps trying to impose taxonomy, doctrine, and command-structure language onto something that has none of those things, it has no strategy, no center, no goal beyond making more of itself, closer to cancer or a Zerg-flavored/cosmic-horror mass than a real war-fighting biology would predict. Early years present that absence of pattern as reassuring ("cannot strategize against us"). Later years, once cadets have actually seen it, that same absence reads as the opposite of reassuring. Never resolve this tension in narration, let the fragments sit against the field chapters and do it themselves.

"Qiraki" never appears in any of these, spoiler lock holds throughout.

## Usage notes

- Keep every fragment under three sentences. Longer starts reading as worldbuilding exposition rather than texture.

- Never follow a fragment with narration that comments on it. The chapter's own content is the commentary.

- Every chapter, LOCKED (see frequency note above). Vary source type (curriculum, poster, broadcast, internal memo, personal correspondence template, month-theme signage, mess-hall notice, family letter, dress code, points-economy messaging) so back-to-back chapters never read as the same kind of document, this is what actually keeps the device from becoming a gimmick now that frequency alone can't.

No-reuse rule, LOCKED, supersedes the earlier "3-4 per book" note.

176 total fragments now in the bank, structured so almost nothing repeats across Books 1-6: month-theme fragments (36, three eras per month, exactly two uses each, 72 total needed, exact match), Bloom curriculum and the new rune-tech/engineering/combat curriculum banks (18 each, 90 total, fully unique, one year, one book, never repeated), and a shared flexible pool for everything else (Hiopi and Osnius cultural texts plus general institutional texture, 63 fragments) covering the roughly 23 remaining chapters per book that aren't tied to a specific month or year. That shared pool averages 2.2 reuses per fragment across the entire six-book run, down from 8.2 before this expansion, spread far enough apart that it shouldn't read as repetition to any reader. Still worth topping up before Book 7's own planning pass, which needs its own material entirely.
