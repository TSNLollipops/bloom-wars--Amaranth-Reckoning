# THE QIRAKI FILES — Path Audit, Books 1-6

*Part of the Qiraki files. Traces every character, thread, and locked execution rule across all 282 academy chapters and reports where a path breaks, thins, or never lands. Findings are ranked by severity, not by book.**** ******Nothing here is fixed.****** ****This is a defect list, and several items are author-lane calls.*

**Method:** every named character in Qiraki_Character_Sheets.md was traced against every chapter in all six outlines. Every thread opened in an outline was traced to its resolution or to its absence. Every locked execution rule was checked for a chapter assignment.

## SEVERITY 1 — Locked rules with nowhere to happen

These are not judgment calls. Each is a rule the project has already committed to that currently has no chapter to occur in.

### 1.1 Reqa’s name correction has no scene

Qiraki_Character_Sheets.md carries a hard execution rule: the misspelling **“************Reaka************”** appears in prose up through the specific scene where she corrects Trav for the last time, that scene uses “Reaka” once more as the final wrong instance, and from that point every appearance is **“************Reqa************”** permanently. The reader is meant to watch the spelling change and stay changed.

**That scene is not assigned to any chapter in any of the six outlines.** It is one of the most concrete, most trackable, most reader-visible rules in the entire project and there is no slot for it.

**It also constrains everything before it.** Until that scene, her name is spelled wrong in prose. Six books of drafting depend on knowing where the switch happens, and B1.CH01 is already drafted.

**Author call required, and it should probably be early.** The rule works best if the reader lives with the wrong spelling long enough to feel it, but every chapter drafted before the decision is a chapter that may need its spelling swept.

### 1.2 The socket from Yssa never lands

B1.CH01: Yssa presses a socket into Trav’s hand and says she doesn’t know what it’s for either. Qiraki_Master_Style_Guide.md Rule 10 explicitly flags it as a legitimate future candidate for the isolated single-line paragraph, the highest-emphasis tool in the project.

**It is never returned to in any of the six books.** An object handed over in the first chapter of a seven-book series, flagged in the style guide as load-bearing, with no payoff anywhere in the academy arc.

**Three shapes available, all author’s call:** it pays off in the academy years (most likely Book 6, the leaving book), it pays off in the military era (stronger, longer fuse), or it deliberately never pays off and is only ever a thing his mother gave him. **The third is defensible and would need to be a stated decision**, because an unpaid object reads as forgotten unless the documents say otherwise. This is exactly what the Dropped-Thread Register exists for.

### 1.3 Perrin Cato is locked to Book 1 and absent from it

His sheet: “Mek, LOCKED: Perrin Cato, human, matched Book 1.” Book 1’s outline does not contain him. He first appears in B4.CH08.

**Cheap fix available.** Books 3, 4, 5 and 6 each have ensemble chapters that could carry a mek beat without a new chapter, which is now standard practice per the merge rule.

### 2.2 Instructor Cassia Vantree appears once

Locked as combat instructor **years 1 through 6**. Her sheet is one of the richest in the project: missing left hand below the wrist, restock-regrown and functionally perfect, seam never hidden, living proof in front of a room of twelve-year-olds of what the training costs and how survivable it is. Runs full-contact wound-emulation from the first Year 1 session. Reads as protective rather than cruel. Teaches by demonstration and correction, RPD in her own bones the way it will eventually be in Trav’s.

**She appears in B1.CH04 and never again in any outline.** Six years of combat instruction, one scene.

**This is the single largest gap between what a sheet establishes and what the outlines use.** She is also the only adult positioned to notice Trav’s development from the inside, and her own open question (“whether she clocks him before he’s obviously exceptional”) has no chapter to be answered in.

**She appears in B1.CH01.** Every book has a weekend-choice chapter (“The Choice”), and none of them specify whether he goes home or who is there.

### 2.6 Soren Adair has a three-book hole — RESOLVED 2026-08-12

Introduced B1.CH05. Previously nothing in Books 2 or 3. Returns B4.CH04, deepened Ch. 16, dies Ch. 25.

Book 4’s outline says the Ch. 4 chapter deepens reader investment “months before Act 3,” which is true within Book 4 and previously missed the larger problem: the reader last saw him two entire books ago. His death is the emotional centerpiece of the academy arc and was resting on a character absent for roughly 94 chapters.

**Fix, logged in full in Qiraki_Character_Sheets.md’s Soren Adair entry.** Four small touchpoints added to the existing Book 2 and Book 3 outlines, no new chapters, no chapter-count or title changes: B2.CH13, B3.CH02, B3.CH04, B3.CH18. Gap between introduction and return drops from roughly 94 chapters to roughly 30. Qiraki_Chapter_Index.md is unaffected and does not need regenerating, since no chapter was added, cut, or reordered.

## SEVERITY 3 — Threads that open and do not close

### 3.1 Krethis’s resentment, six books unexplained

Opened B2.CH03, unexplained by design. Recurs B2.CH28, B4.CH32, B5.CH28, B6.CH28. Book 6’s own outline describes it as “six books old now and still unresolved.”

The two-part trope discipline requires in-book resolution **and** downstream echo. This has neither, across the entire academy arc. **Either it resolves in Book 7, or it is deliberately a permanent condition, and one of those needs writing down.**

### 3.2 Two Book 2 tropes have no downstream echo

- **Cross-year mentorship (B2.CH08).** Outline states it resolves within the book. No downstream callback is planted anywhere.

- **The wash-out (B2.CH21-22).** A cadet leaves. No echo anywhere in Books 3-6.

Both violate the standing rule that no trope is used once and abandoned.

### 3.3 Book 6’s underdog trope has no stated in-book resolution

B6.CH16 draws the underdog trope as an echo of B1.CH23. The downstream half is satisfied. **The in-book half is not specified**, which is the exact gap the two-part rule was created to catch.

### 3.4 The symbiosis pain has no Book 6 beat

B3.CH08 begins, Ch. 18 worsens, Ch. 45 unresolved. B4.CH02 worse. B5.CH09 still present. **Book 6: nothing.**

The Points Shop Catalog locks Ironmind (B-tier, military era) as the eventual answer and explicitly says it arrives well after the roughest years rather than fixing them retroactively. So Book 6 silence may be correct. **But it needs to be a decision:** does it plateau, fade with maturity, or is he simply used to it? Currently it just stops being mentioned, which reads as dropped.

### 4.4 Act lengths and calendar months do not align evenly

Acts run 12 / 11 / 13 / 11. Months run Jan-Mar (3), Apr-Jul (4), Aug-Oct (3), Nov-Dec (2). So Act 2 covers four months in eleven chapters and Act 4 covers two months in eleven. Act 4’s density is defensible, it is exam and ritual season. **Act 2 is the thin one**, four months at roughly 2.75 chapters per month, and it is also where most books put their least-structured material.

**One defect already found and fixed by this pattern:** B5.CH22 was titled “Ambition,” an August theme sitting inside an April-July act, duplicating Ch. 24. Corrected to Gratitude, July’s actual theme.
