# THE QIRAKI FILES — Book 3, "The Choosing," Chapter-by-Chapter Outline

*Part of the Qiraki files. Same template and standing rules as Book 1's outline. Cadets are 14-15 this book.*

**Locked spine:** class specialization formally locks, path-system (points shop) access opens, first real injury, symbiosis's pain-spike begins. Combat class expands from Friday/Saturday to Thursday-Saturday, the schedule change that creates real multi-day separation between Trav and Reqa for the first time.

**Curriculum, Year 3:** rune-patterning reaches Operations, knots properly taught, real boolean logic (AND/OR/NOT built from how many traces converge at a peg), and the curriculum splits by path for the first time. Combat class runs First Cut, the first genuinely path-differentiated coursework. Engineering moves toward fluid power and materials.

**Emotional target:** quiet fear (the pain-spike, unexplained and unexamined, the reader feeling it before Trav would ever name it), smile (the joke-ritual finding its new home), cry (the year's locked first real injury), relief (Denic's Munti path validated in the worst possible way).

## Act 1 — The lock (ch. 1-12, January-March)

**Governing psychology:** mid-adolescence proper, identity consolidating around what each kid is now formally *for*, and the first real experience of a decision that can't be walked back.

- **Three Days.** The combat schedule expands to Thursday-Saturday. Disruptive in a good way, the whole cohort adjusting to a harder rhythm. Immediately creates the double-rune and double-engineering days that drive Reqa's crankiness (and, on the mirror day, her favorite day of the week).

- **The Lock.** Formal specialization chapter, callback to Book 2's informal debate. Each of the five commits: Trav Meeps, Corw Tank, Ilyen Reeps, Talia Reeps, Denic Munti. Real weight, genuinely not easily reversed. **Soren beat:** commits Meeps in the same beat, off to the side of Trav's own five, direct payoff of B2.CH13's seed. No dialogue needed, a nod across the room does it.

- **The Shop Opens.** Points-shop access unlocks (years 1-3 per the existing lock). G-tier purchases within reach for anyone actually training. Trav gets his Stocklance, standard-issue, unremarkable, and the economy becomes visible to the reader as a real system.

- **First Cut.** New combat coursework, path-differentiated for the first time, a Meeps cadet's footwork drills look nothing like a Tank's positioning drills from this point forward. The cohort physically separates during class in a way it never has. **Soren beat:** path-differentiated coursework now puts every Meeps cadet in the same room regardless of team, Soren included, the structurally honest reason he's back in scenes with Trav without either of them being on the same five-pilot roster.

- **Knots.** Year 3 rune-patterning, real boolean logic, the first material that behaves differently depending on what it reads. Strong reader-facing curriculum content.

- **The New Gap.** The schedule change's real social cost, Trav and Reqa genuinely separated across multiple days for the first time. The joke-ritual extends into the new gap, landing after combat class rather than after bad rune days, an already-automatic habit generalizing rather than a new one invented under pressure.

- **Quickstep.** First genetic/stimulant upgrade purchases ripple through the cohort, real status attached to being early. *Psych: at this age, visible status markers among peers matter enormously, and a reaction-time patch is exactly that.*

- **Something Wrong.** The symbiosis pain-spike begins. Trav feels it more acutely, not less, and doesn't examine it, consistent with his non-reflective characterization. The reader should feel the wrongness he doesn't. **Hard constraint: never internally explained, only shown.**

- **First Blood, Year Three.** Vrassik clash, first genuinely damaging exchange of the Years 3-4 stretch. Two close-range Meeps in constant real proximity, the mechanical reason this year hurts.

- **Study Session I.** Trav and Reqa, subject: Structural Read, the manual academy-era schematic transfer, a good on-page moment of Reqa walking his schematic over by hand.

- **The Path Not Taken.** A cadet who chose badly and knows it, the first real consequence of Ch. 2's irreversibility. Resolves in-book.

- **The Choice.** Weekend-choice chapter, the found family now competing against home in a way it didn't at twelve.

## Act 2 — The body under the training (ch. 13-23, April-July)

**Governing psychology:** the associative stage of motor learning, clumsier and more error-prone than either the year before or after, and the frustration of getting visibly worse before getting better.

- **Worse Before Better.** The whole cohort's performance dips as harder material meets bodies still in the least reliable learning stage. Real, documented, and worth showing honestly rather than as a slump anyone explains.

- **Equipment Failure.** Trope draw, something fails at the worst possible moment, human error or institutional cost-cutting behind it. Resolves in-book.

- **Study Session II.** Engineering-side, real Year 3 content.

- **The Ritual, Relocated.** The joke-ritual's new post-combat-class placement pays off for the first time in a chapter that actually needs it, Reqa cranky, Trav delivering, the machinery of their friendship visibly working.

- **Talia, Grinding.** Talia-focused chapter, her method (repetition, no talent) visibly working while Trav's reflexes carry him past her anyway. Her friction gets its most honest airing yet without curdling.

- **Pain, Again.** Second pain-spike beat, worse, still unexamined. Denic notices something and doesn't have the words for it either. **Soren beat, load-bearing:** also Meeps, also feeling it, mentions it to Trav in passing the same way anyone mentions a shared minor ailment, both of them treating it as ordinary because neither has anything to compare it against. Reads as nothing at the time. No narration should mark it as anything else. Reads as everything once B4.CH04 lands, per the standing rule against stated theses, the weight comes entirely from the reader's own memory of this exact exchange, never from the text pointing back at it.

- **Ilyen Struggles.** Reeps demands real piloting even at range, and it isn't her natural strength. First honest look at the problem that Book 5 eventually corrects. **Second tell, LOCKED:** the one thing that visibly steadies her mid-struggle is Trav dropping by unprompted with food neither of them has time for, she doesn't ask him to, doesn't thank him more than the moment requires, but the next bad day, she's noticeably watching the door before he shows up. Trav reads it as ordinary team loyalty, same as anything else he'd do for Denic or Corw. It isn't, quite, to her.

- **June.** Ensemble day, first Year 3 Bloom fragment lands, the "no central nervous system, no command structure" curriculum line.

- **The Accident.** Not the year's big injury, a smaller one, someone hurt badly enough to matter. Restock handles it. The Spawn Line and the arena safety system do their job, visibly.

- **Second Blood.** Vrassik again, escalating, both of them now carrying real marks from each other.

- **Midpoint.** Ensemble chapter, the team closer than they were, and visibly more banged up.

## Act 3 — The injury (ch. 24-36, August-October)

**Governing psychology:** the first time this cohort experiences an injury the system doesn't cleanly absorb, and the specific fear of watching a friend not get up.

- **Heavylance.** F-tier purchases land, Trav's weapon actually changes for the first time, real in-fiction preference forming (weight, reach, how it feels), not a purchase dictating his style.

- **Ambition.** August's theme, rivalry sharpest, Trav/Corw and Vrassik both escalate.

- **The Test That Goes Sideways.** "Prove yourself" trope, real consequence.

- **Study Session III.** Third session, unused subject.

- **Cross-Cohort.** Vrassik's team and Trav's in shared exercise. Lissrak's Tank work visibly opening lanes for Vrassik specifically, devotion legible only in retrospect, nobody watching has to know it means more to her than to anyone else on the field.

- **Unity.** September, cultural exchange, runway toward Homecoming.

- **The Real Injury.** The year's locked first real injury lands. Full gritty-gorgeous weight, no rushing past it. Trav or a core teammate, author's call at draft time.

- **Extraction.** Denic's Munti path gets its first genuine showcase, an actual high-stakes recovery. The moment his path choice stops being a surprise and starts being why everyone's glad he's on the team.

- **Restock.** The aftermath, the callous soldier-slang term meeting a fourteen-year-old's actual body. *Psych: the gap between institutional language and lived experience is exactly the kind of critique this project never states out loud.*

- **Vigilance.** October, second and third Year 3 Bloom fragments, including the instructor's private margin note about something with no brain never attacking the same way twice.

- **The Team Closes.** The unit rallying around the injured member, found-family payoff earned rather than asserted.

- **Corw's Year.** Corw's Tank identity starting to actually suit him, the ego-test arc turning a corner without resolving.

- **Countdown.** Year 3's exam format explained, smaller-scale than Books 1-2's set pieces, a checkpoint rather than a spectacle, worth keeping proportionate.

## Act 4 — Reckoning season (ch. 37-47, November-December)

- **Homecoming, Third Year.** The team's traditions now genuinely their own.

- **The Ritual.** Third Hiopi ritual, the cohort's reactions now split by how much each of them has personally bled this year.

- **The Exam, Day One.** Year 3's assessment, deliberately less monumental than King of the Hill or Capture the Flag.

- **The Exam, Day Two.**

- **The Exam, Final.** Placements land, rank changes real.

- **What Changed.** Aftermath, the specialization lock's first full-year verdict on who chose well.

- **Twinlance.** E-tier reached by the strongest performers, Trav's second lance issued, the real coordination cost of it, weeks of fumbling the offhand ahead of him.

- **Kinship.** December, quiet, the team's shared season, the injured member fully back.

- **The Pain, Still There.** Final pain-spike beat of the year, unresolved, unexamined, carried into Book 4. Deliberately not answered.

- **Loose Threads.** Calm chapter, the cohort settling.

- **Bridge.** Closes Year 3, opens directly into Book 4, the roughest year, with the pain already escalating and Vrassik already bleeding.
