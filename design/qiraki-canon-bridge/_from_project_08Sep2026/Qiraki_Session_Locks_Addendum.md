## CORRECTION: the suppression mechanism was already locked

I presented this as new material. **It is not.** Qiraki_Concept.md already carries it, in the academy daily-structure section:

*Fertility control, LOCKED, dry dark comedy: actual pregnancy essentially doesn't happen at the academy because the food is, bluntly, drugged. Played matter-of-fact rather than horrified, an unstated institutional fact of life rather than a plot point anyone reacts to.*

That lock is already correct and already says "unstated." **The only genuinely new part is the execution rule below**, and it is the only thing that needs adding to Concept.md.

### Execution rule, the actual addition

**Never explained during the academy books.** Not by narration, not by a character, not by a propaganda fragment. The reader notices, if at all, that nobody in six books has time or inclination, and the books never draw attention to it.

**The reveal is military era.** Trav works out what was in the food years later, as an adult, with nobody having told him. That is the only placement where it functions as horror rather than exposition, and the only placement that keeps the academy books from having narration that discusses adolescent sexuality in books whose entire cast are minors. **The craft reason and the content reason point the same way**, which is usually a sign the placement is right.

**Corollary:** the Trav/Reqa romance beginning years after graduation, already locked for other reasons, now has a second independent mechanism supporting it. Don't over-explain that either. Two locks pointing at one outcome is enough.

### Ilyen, same rule applied

Her feelings across Books 2-6 stay exactly as locked: five escalating tells, legible to the reader, invisible to Trav, entirely platonic on the page, nothing physical and nothing named. **If she ever acts on it, it is military era and both are adults**, and it arrives as organic evolution of someone who spent six years quietly tracking one person and finally has a life where that is allowed to mean something. Not a lurch, and not a scene the earlier books were secretly building toward. The academy version of that feeling is complete in itself, unrequited, unstated, never leveraged.

## Dialogue realism exemption, LOCKED, new 2026-08-16

Style Guide constraints aimed at narration (word minimization on "thing"/"stuff"/"true," no repeated construction, no symmetry) are narration-side defaults, not blanket prose rules applying equally to everything on the page. Dialogue gets more latitude for the sake of realism, real speech repeats words, hedges, and leans on filler in ways that would read as sloppy in Trav's narration but read as authentic coming out of a character's mouth. Not unlimited: still governed by each character's own voice lock, and dialogue isn't a dumping ground for banned words just because it's technically exempt, the latitude is for naturalism, not license. Applied retroactively: Ch.3's Hiopi breakfast line ("nobody carries anything") stays as-is despite the narration-side "Nobody" cluster elsewhere in the same chapter being trimmed down. **Clarifies, doesn't contradict, the Master Style Guide's read-aloud-flow section, fold in there on the next full sync rather than treating this as a second, competing rule.**
