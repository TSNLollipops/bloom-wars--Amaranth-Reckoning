# THE QIRAKI FILES — Book 2, "Flags and Foundations," Chapter-by-Chapter Outline

*Part of the Qiraki files. Same template as Book 1's outline, see that file for the standing rules (instructor ratio, propaganda frequency, daily-rhythm classification) which govern here identically and are not restated in full. Cadets are 13-14 this book.*

**Locked spine:** Capture the Flag exam, first institutional friction, class-choice debate chapter, team assembles.

**Curriculum, Year 2:** rune-patterning reaches Sequence (circuits, multiple traces linked and read in order, no branching yet). The Trav/Reqa romance is locked to begin years after graduation. **Ilyen's crush, LOCKED craft approach:** stays unnoticed by Trav specifically, his non-reflective POV never editorializes about it, that's the actual engine, but the reader gets to see it through her own small, specific, factually-narrated actions across these books, classic dramatic irony, legible-feelings-only, nothing romantic or physical on the page given both are minors throughout. ## Act 1 — Return and reshuffle (ch. 1-12, January-March)

**Governing psychology:** second-year confidence, kids who now know the system well enough to have opinions about it, and the first real awareness that they're being sorted rather than simply taught.

- **Second January.** Return after Kinship, the academy familiar now, deliberate contrast against Book 1's Ch. 1 arrival. Discipline month opens with a harder training ramp everyone recognizes this time.

- **New Faces.** Talia Renn steps forward from the wider cohort into Trav's orbit, matched with Sana Voight around the same time. **Loop-opening beats, Talia and Sana.** Her established friction with Trav starts here, honest rather than petty, things coming easily to him that cost her real work.

- **The Other Roster.** Off Trav's page mostly, Vrassik's own cohort going through the same team-formation year, Bruvald Ashe and Krethis introduced. **Loop-opening beats, Bruvald and Krethis.** Krethis's specific resentment (a human excelling at something that started as a Hiopi rite) seeded here, unexplained, just present.

- **Circuits.** Year 2 rune-patterning opens, sequence introduced, the first material that actually *does* something rather than just being read. Real curriculum content, reader-facing.

- **Maze Runs.** New combat coursework, small-unit drills inside a scaled-down maze, direct rehearsal for the year's exam. First time the cohort works in groups rather than the Spawn Line's individual bouts.

- **The Guardian, Second Year.** Academy-tier AI relationship deepens, cadets now fluent in working with and around it. Callback to Book 1's Ch. 9.

- **What Money Buys.** First institutional friction lands: a legacy-track cadet's "standard-issue" gear turns out to be quietly better than everyone else's baseline. Nobody says the word unfair out loud. *Psych: fairness judgments are sharp and absolute at this age, which is exactly what makes the adults' non-reaction land.*

- **Cross-Year.** Cross-mentorship trope, an older cadet takes an interest in Trav or Denic, motive genuinely ambiguous, resolves within this book per trope discipline.

- **The Weight Room.** Ordinary daily-rhythm chapter, the cohort's physical training visibly harder this year, found-family texture deepening.

- **Study Session I.** Trav and Reqa, subject: the Warning-Light Circuit, engineering-side, paired against rune-patterning's Diagnostic Ping as two disciplines solving the same problem.

- **Second Blood, Second Year.** Vrassik clash, escalated from Book 1's scale but still short of Years 3-4's real damage.

- **The Choice, Again.** Weekend-choice chapter, different stakes now that a real peer group exists to disappoint.

## Act 2 — The debate and the shape of a team (ch. 13-23, April-July)

**Governing psychology:** identity formation through choice, the first genuinely consequential decision most of these kids have ever made, and the social sorting that follows it.

- **Four Paths.** The class-choice debate chapter, a real set piece. Meeps, Tank, Reeps, Munti each pitched and roasted by thirteen-year-olds with far more conviction than information. Comedy high point of the book. **Soren beat, new, resolves Qiraki_Path_Audit_Books1-6.md §2.6:** Soren Adair, Trav's B1.CH05 bond from the shared illiteracy panic, speaks up for Meeps, not because of Trav, because it's the path where you're just supposed to hit the thing in front of you, refreshingly simple against Reeps and Tank's more visible specialist pressure. One line, plants B3.CH02's payoff rather than letting it land cold.

- **After the Debate.** Informal choices start landing. Ilyen picks Reeps here, ahead of Book 3's formal lock. **First tell, LOCKED:** she volunteers for whatever lab slot overlaps with Trav's own schedule, framed plainly as "better logistics, less time wasted," true as far as it goes, and slightly too convenient for anyone paying attention. Trav doesn't clock it. *Psych: at this age, choices get made for social reasons and rationalized afterward, worth writing honestly rather than as clean self-knowledge.*

- **Assembly.** The team finishes forming around Trav: Corw, Ilyen, Denic, Talia, five pilots, five meks. The title's actual "Foundations." Ensemble chapter, real warmth.

- **Denic's Choice.** Denic drifts toward Munti, and nobody expects it, loud and combat-gifted reads Meeps or Tank on paper. Unremarked-on, the reader assembling it before the institution names it.

- **The Rumor Mill, Sophomore Edition.** Rumor-spiral trope, this time about the path choices themselves, who's "wasting" their aptitude.

- **Study Session II.** Trav and Reqa, subject: circuit sequence, real reader-facing rune-tech content.

- **First Team Failure.** The new five-pilot unit runs a Maze Run together and does badly, real friction, real blame. *Psych: group cohesion at this age forms through shared failure at least as much as shared success.*

- **June.** Ordinary ensemble day, first Year 2 Bloom curriculum fragment lands (Curiosity month). **Padded with:** Talia and Trav's friction getting its first real airing rather than staying background.

- **The Wash-Out, Part One.** A classmate the reader has met begins visibly struggling with wound-emulation itself, not combat skill, the interface. Handled straight, no reassurance offered.

- **The Wash-Out, Part Two.** They leave. Given real weight, a full chapter, not a footnote. *Psych: the first time this cohort learns the system can simply remove someone, and that nobody stops it.*

- **Midpoint.** Act 2 closer, the team sitting with what just happened, quieter, every relationship gets a beat.

## Act 3 — Escalation toward the flag (ch. 24-36, August-October)

**Governing psychology:** the cohort now understands it's competing, and the found-family bonds start having to coexist with that, which is genuinely hard at this age.

- **Practice Runs.** Capture the Flag preparation begins in earnest, the maze's yearly-changing door logic introduced as something to be read rather than memorized.

- **The Enforced Rule.** Trope draw: a rule everyone breaks suddenly gets enforced on one specific cadet. Resolves within this book, the unfairness either exposed or absorbed, something happens on the page.

- **Ambition.** August's theme month, sharpest rivalry energy, Trav/Corw and the Vrassik thread both escalate.

- **Study Session III.** Third study session, a subject not yet used.

- **Cross-Cohort.** Trav's team and Vrassik's in the same exercise, Jifsook and Lissrak get real page time this year rather than Book 1's brief seed. Krethis's resentment surfaces sideways, a refusal to celebrate something the rest of the room celebrates.

- **Unity.** September's cultural-exchange month, interspecies texture, deliberate runway toward Homecoming.

- **Fenn, Doyle, Petra, Sana.** Mek-side chapter, the four of them working the same problem, meks visibly outpacing pilots technically (per the locked schedule mechanism, meks get solo workshop time on combat days). Real payoff for a group that mostly works offscreen.

- **Vigilance.** October opens, second and third Year 2 Bloom fragments land, including the instructor's unscripted "fifth kind" aside.

- **Corw, Slipping.** Corw's confidence takes a real hit as the team format starts rewarding things he isn't best at. Seeds his Book 3+ ego arc.

- **Reqa's Workshop.** Reqa-focused chapter, her technical instincts and her genuine preference for engineering over rune-patterning both sharpening.

- **The Legacy Cadet.** Payoff on Ch. 7's resource gap, the legacy-track cadet gets a real, specific advantage in a graded context and everyone sees it. Resolves in-book without being fixed.

- **The Cohort Holds.** Ensemble chapter, every relationship gets a beat before the exam.

- **Countdown.** Capture the Flag's format and stakes laid out from the cadets' side.

- **The Ritual, Known.** Second Hiopi ritual, no longer bewildering, deliberate contrast against Book 1's outsider-POV version. Vrassik competes and doesn't win, groundwork for Book 4.

- **Capture the Flag, Day One.** Week-long exam begins, first genuinely team-based assessment.

- **Capture the Flag, Day Two.**

- **Capture the Flag, Day Three.** The new team's first real test as a unit, callback to Ch. 19's failure.

- **Capture the Flag, Day Four.**

- **Capture the Flag, Final.** Exam concludes, placements land.

- **What Held.** Aftermath, who performed, what the team learned about itself.

- **Kinship.** December, quieter, the team's first shared holiday season as an actual team.

- **Loose Threads.** Deliberately calm, the cohort settling.

- **Bridge.** Closes Year 2, opens into Book 3's specialization lock and the schedule change.
