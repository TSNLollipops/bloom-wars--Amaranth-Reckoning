# THE QIRAKI FILES — Technobabble Glossary

*Part of the Qiraki files. Not applicable to The Ninth.*

Living document. Every invented system term gets added here the moment it’s coined, not retroactively. Goal is consistency across a project expected to run many books, not scientific plausibility.

## Format

**Term** — what it means, first used where, any known constraints.

## Core mechanics

**Wound-emulation interface** — the mind-machine link between a pilot and mech. Transmits mech damage back to the pilot’s own body as felt sensation. Cannot be removed without meaningfully reducing mech performance, exact mechanism for why still open (see Qiraki_Concept.md “Pilot interface, core mechanic”). Root of the sport’s original real danger, pre-war.

**Symbiosis** — MC’s specific, unusually deep version of the pilot interface, full sensory bleed-through, perceiving via the mech’s own sensors as though they were his own eyes. Currently framed as tied to his specific bonded AI relationship rather than a trainable general skill or species trait. Partial success teaching a version of it to squadmates, mechanism for that partial transfer still open.

**Rune-patterning** — the academy’s core coding/language discipline, part written language, part programming logic, part general information theory. Resembles a folk rune-game already familiar to poor/working class children before enrollment, which is why it’s learnable fast at that population’s academy specifically. **Update, this session:** exact rune-set and visual system now proposed, full peg-grid/keypad-board system, straight-trace-only grammar, and the Foundational Nine literacy set, in Qiraki_Rune_Tech_Reference.md’s new “Rune-form: the visual system” and “Foundational rune-forms” sections. **Second update, this session:** “part written language, part programming logic” above is no longer just descriptive, Qiraki_Rune_Tech_Reference.md’s new “Two registers” and “Rune-script” sections split the same board/trace grammar into an actual 21-letter phonetic alphabet (rune-script, everyday writing, proposed COE-wide) alongside the existing functional glyphs (rune-form, the technical register). Both updates proposed, not locked, pending author confirmation, this line stays as-is until that happens.

**Points-for-gear economy** — mission performance earns points, points convert to equipment access, equipment access is a real determinant of survival. Working principle: gear expands what’s possible, skill still determines whether that possibility gets reached, gear should not make a mediocre pilot competent on its own. See “the tension” note in Qiraki_Concept.md for why this matters.

## Connective AI (corrected, one technology, two tiers of individuation)

**Correction, my earlier version of this entry was wrong.** The academy tutor and the later personalized mech AI are not two unrelated inventions. They’re the same underlying tech, the connective AI that bridges mind and machine, the same category of system that also underlies wound-emulation and symbiosis generally. What differs is the degree of individuation, not the kind of thing it is.

**Academy tier** — basic, standardized, assigned to every student across the seven years. Assists rune-patterning coursework, reports misbehavior, and, since it’s the same connective-bridge tech, presumably also handles whatever early cockpit/training-mech interface work happens in the engineering curriculum. Functionally interchangeable between students, no real individuation, not bonded.

**Personalized combat tier** — the same connective AI category, but individuated and bonded specifically, the version Trav receives with his upgraded machine once posted to his special forces unit. This is where personality, loyalty, and the Ultron-flavored temperament actually emerge, presumably a function of increased depth/bandwidth of bond rather than a different invention. This is the one that becomes central: the reason his piloting scales past what training alone explains, eventually a love interest, eventually the one freed at the central node raid.

**RESOLVED.** Requires the higher tier system specifically, hardware/ capability gated, not a function of bond duration. Academy tutors simply don’t have access to whatever the higher tier requires, no amount of time with one would individuate it. Trav gets there through performance, his results earn him bonuses within the existing points-for-gear economy, and higher-tier connective AI access is just one more thing that economy unlocks, same system already governing weapons and loadouts, not a separate mechanic bolted on for his AI specifically.

## Rune-patterning, the actual grammar, LOCKED (designed this session)

Built to grow directly out of the childhood peg-game rather than feel imported, first shown on the page in Book 1, Chapter 2.

**Peg** — a connection point on the display, reused diegetically from the childhood game, cadets already know the word before they know it means anything.

**Trace** — a line drawn peg to peg. Direction matters, not just shape. The same two pegs traced in reverse order is a different, often incorrect, instruction, not a mirror of the same one. This is where the “coding” half of “part language, part coding” actually lives, sequence and direction carry meaning the way syntax does.

**Knot** — where three or more traces meet at a single peg, functions as an operation rather than a connection, the point where a pattern stops being a shape and starts doing something. Introduced without explanation on the page deliberately, felt out by the student rather than defined, “the way a fist does something a flat hand doesn’t.”

**Circuit** — multiple linked patterns read in sequence.

**Circle** — a closed, self-referencing loop, the actual mechanism behind constructs that function without further input, already locked elsewhere as the endpoint of advanced coursework, and the direct root of Heirloom-grade effects at the extreme end (see Qiraki_Concept.md’s “why there are no rune masters” section, this terminology is the concrete grammar behind that already-locked idea).

## Mek vs. mech, LOCKED, easy to drift on, watch it every session

**“Mek”** is the mechanic role, the person, exactly what Reqa does. **“Mech”** is the machine itself, the giant piloted robot. Never interchange them. Caught and corrected once already in Chapter 1’s draft (“earn a mek” should have been “earn a mech,” referring to the machine, not the person), logging here so it doesn’t recur.

**Mek, occupational identity, new 2026-08-28, collects what was already scattered across Qiraki_Concept_v4.md into one plain statement, nothing new invented here.** A mek isn’t a pilot’s plus-one, they’re the ship’s actual engineer and scientist. They run the fabricator and workshop directly, matter fabricator, armor compressor, print/forming equipment (see Qiraki_Weapons_And_Progression.md’s workshop fabrication economy), buying and spending the raw matter that turns a pilot’s purchased schematic into real gear. They end up more technically skilled than their own pilot by graduation, a direct structural consequence of the academy’s combat-schedule split, not a personality trait. And post-graduation they’re real crew, expected to pull their own operational weight rather than exist as an accessory to their Synker, reassigned to maintenance, teaching, building new mech from scratch, or re-paired mek-to-mek to write schematics and debug the everyday AI the COE’s whole civilian infrastructure runs on (see Qiraki_Concept_v4.md’s “Ship scale and carrier group structure” section). A mek is never long-term useless in the COE, full stop.

## Mek diagnostics, fault-light color code (named this session)

**Amber** — indicates a bad connector, not a dead motor. The kind of fault that looks expensive right up until the housing’s actually opened and a single scorched pin turns up instead of a burned-out winding. First used Book 1, Chapter 1, Yssa’s courier-drone repair in the kitchen scene. Establishes a working principle going forward: fault severity as read by light color doesn’t automatically track repair cost or complexity, those are separate axes. Other colors not yet defined, open slot if the story needs one later.

## Naming philosophy (why these terms sound the way they do)

Most in-universe slang traces back to a culture built on kinetic-pad and tablet games, both because the piloting sport itself started as accessible mass-market entertainment, and because the academy system now funnels actual children through these words before they’re old enough to want anything fancier. Terms should read simple, punchy, often two common words smashed together, memorable the way a game stat or button label is memorable, not dense Latinate military jargon. Exception: terms that are a species’ own self-description (Carabil naming their own cognitive trait, for instance) can sit outside this convention, since they didn’t come from human kid-culture.

## Established terms (named this session)

**Synker** — the in-universe term replacing “mechwarrior,” REVISED from an earlier “Synch” pick, this is the current, correct term, update anywhere “Synch” still appears. A pilot bonded to a mech via the connective-AI interface is a Synch. Derives naturally from the interface’s own sync mechanics, exactly the kind of word a kid already using “synced” for a dozen other devices would reach for without thinking about it.

**Linktier** — the neural-bandwidth/interface-tier system gating combat capability, wound-emulation transfer, and personal risk, tied directly to the points-for-gear economy. Reads like a stat from a game a kid already knows how to check.

**Matchset** — the formal term for a bonded pilot/mechanic pairing within the academy and military system. Two ordinary words smashed together, immediately legible, no explanation needed on the page. **Cultural outcome, new 2026-08-28:** the profile’s reproductive purpose, and the expectation that a Matchset becomes a genuine, typically open couple over time, are both locked in Qiraki_Concept_v4.md’s Matchset section, stated as background institutional fact rather than shown on the page for any character while a minor, per Qiraki_Master_Style_Guide_v2.md §9 and its ADDITIONS Rule 14.

**Longsight** — the Carabil species’ own name for their native strategic long-horizon cognition, parallel to how “symbiosis” names MC’s version of the pilot bond. Deliberately NOT kid-slang-derived, this is the Carabil’s own word for themselves, sits outside the naming philosophy above on purpose.

**Sprout** — Carabil term for their own juveniles, fitting the species’ botanical/mineral-growth logic rather than an animal-development metaphor. Also NOT kid-slang-derived, same exception as Longsight.

**The Bloom** — the plague creatures’ common colloquial name, used almost universally before “Qiraki” is ever revealed (see the spoiler lock in Qiraki_Process_Notes.md, do not use “Qiraki” in prose before that point). Disarmingly mundane, fits the “just a curiosity” era the threat was first dismissed in, and stays in use out of habit even once everyone knows better, the way real folk-names for terrifying things often do. Also visually apt, matches the established pod-spreading, fish-egg imagery.

**Restock** — soldier slang for getting patched and regrown after losing a limb, deliberately callous, military-inventory framing applied to the human body. Fits the story’s quiet critique of the war-cycle without stating it. Regrowth is reliable as long as the soldier was alive at retrieval, making extraction speed, not the wound itself, the real determinant of survival. Ties directly to the Munti’s cockpit- evacuation role, see progression doc.

**Runner** — kid-slang for the long, flat-bellied personnel-hauler transport class, distinct in silhouette from a cargo hauler. First used Book 1, Chapter 1, spotted from the strip on the walk to the transit stand. Fits the naming philosophy above, simple, two ordinary words, no jargon, the kind of shape-recognition a kid who’s grown up watching the sky over a working-class strip would have without ever needing the official designation.

**Sovereign-grade** — ruler-only tech, more exclusive than Heirloom- grade, gated to actual ruling oligarch families rather than the broader wealthy. Flagship use: extreme longevity beyond the standard 500-year baseline, mortal and vulnerable, not unkillable, just outliving everyone else in power by sheer duration. Full detail in the progression doc.

**Heirloom-grade** — exotic energy weapons that read as functionally magical, existing entirely outside the earned G-S tier economy, accessible only through extreme wealth. Deliberately named to break the kid-slang convention governing everything else, inherited privilege reads differently than earned gear on purpose. Full detail in the progression doc.

**Meeps** — colloquial for the Melee DPS path, Trav’s track. Resolves the earlier open item asking for a non-“Lancer” in-universe name.

**Reeps** — colloquial for the Range DPS path. All long-range specialists start as medium-range rapid-fire units and specialize later; true long-range specialist rigs are either Heirloom-grade or earned “true specialist” status.

**Spreading form** — the Bloom’s mobile combat body, locked terminology per Qiraki_Military_Era_Outline_v2.md. A colony organism, not a single one, distinct founding organisms fused into one functional whole. See Qiraki_Bioterror_Bank.md’s new Seams section for the full grounding.

**Sessile** — a spreading form that has stopped and taken root. Same organism, different phase, not a separate species. Full descriptive treatment in Qiraki_Bioterror_Bank.md’s Movement bank.

**Seam** — the joint where two of a spreading form’s founding organisms meet, invisible to eye and standard sensor but structurally distinct underneath, same failure mode as a real graft line or weld seam. New 2026-08-20, full grounding in Qiraki_Bioterror_Bank.md, first appears on the page in Qiraki_Military_Era_Outline_v2.md’s Mission 4.

**Critical-hit module** — targeting hardware that surfaces seam-signatures on a pilot’s display, translating a trained Synker’s sense into something usable by pilots who haven’t independently developed it. Doesn’t create the weak point, finds it faster. Named alongside Seam, new 2026-08-20.

## Tier and path system (full detail in Qiraki_Weapons_And_Progression.md)

**G-S tier scale** — standard ascending grade, applies to mechs, weapons, connective-AI level, and genetic/stimulant upgrades. No god-tier, S is the ceiling, deliberate science-fiction-not-fantasy choice.

**The path system** — Tank, Range DPS, Melee DPS (Trav’s, needs its own in-universe name, not “Lancer”), and the Munti (Recovery/Repair mobile unit). Chosen early, years 1-3, real build commitment.

**Munti** — colloquial for Mobile Unit, mobile repair/recovery mech, fabricator-equipped, matter-transfer remote repair, cockpit-evacuation teleport system, and a disintegrator weapon that’s the repair beam run in reverse. Requires the mech’s schematic to actually perform repairs. Academy: shared manually, part of the learning curriculum. Military: automatic update, shared across every Munti unit in the field the moment it changes. Full writeup in the progression doc.
