# THE QIRAKI FILES — Preserve Species Roster

*Part of the Qiraki files. Pre-spacefaring species living within Coalition
territory under "preserve" status, protected native zones the COE built
around rather than through. Most exist here to draw a deliberate parallel
with what Zeteii later builds versus how the COE has actually treated
peoples like these, per the author's explicit intent. Trav doesn't learn
about the preserve system until after freeing Zeteii. Most entries get a
line or two, not full development, that's the point, a whole civilization
reduced to a footnote is itself the argument.*

1. **The Kelvinshoal** — aquatic, bioluminescent, communicate via light
   patterns across open water. Preserve is a single moon-sized ocean
   world, no surface land at all.
2. **The Ashen Choir** — fungal collective intelligence, individual
   "bodies" are fruiting structures of one shared root network. No
   concept of individual death, only local pruning.
3. **Vethrik-kin** — avian, flightless, built for endurance walking
   across their homeworld's supercontinent. Migratory culture, no fixed
   settlements at contact.
4. **The Orlum Choir** — name coincidence with the Ashen Choir noted and
   deliberate, they're unrelated, silicate-based, extremely slow
   metabolism, COE survey teams initially misclassified them as
   geological formations for eleven years.
5. **Ptessa-need** — small, burrowing, eusocial, single reproductive
   caste per colony. COE agricultural interest in their soil-processing
   biology is a quiet, ongoing point of tension.
6. **The Halewrought** — amphibious, semi-industrial (basic metallurgy,
   no powered flight), the most technologically advanced preserve
   species, which makes their preserve status the most visibly punitive
   rather than protective.
7. **Corvane drift-clans** — nomadic, live on migratory megafauna rather
   than in settlements, no fixed preserve boundary, a legal headache the
   Cradle Circle has quietly never resolved.
8. **The Ilyu** — small, arboreal, four-armed, first contact went badly
   for reasons buried in the same unremembered history as the Hiopi/
   Jhubi conquest chain.
9. **Serrat-bound** — crystalline growth species, related in aesthetic
   only, not biology, to the Carabil, a coincidence that gets remarked
   on exactly once and never explained.
10. **The Hollow Kin** — subterranean, blind, echolocative, entire
    preserve is underground, surface access restricted even to them by
    treaty.
11. **Vann-thet** — plains herd species, pack-social, closest COE
    analogue is a hooved mammal, though they're neither.
12. **The Quiet Root** — sessile as adults, motile only in a brief larval
    stage, meaning "contact" with an adult Quiet Root individual is
    permanent and singular.
13. **Ozhrel-kin** — desert, water-storing, culture built entirely
    around drought cycles, preserve status controversial since COE
    terraforming nearby keeps altering their native rainfall patterns.
14. **The Marrow Choir** — third "Choir"-named species on this list,
    also unrelated to the other two, naming coincidence never explained
    in-universe either, a running joke worth keeping.
15. **Tessin-var** — small flying colonial organisms, individually
    non-sapient, sapience only emerges at swarm scale, a preserve
    species that's arguably closer to the Bloom's own logic than
    anything else on this list, worth flagging as thematically loaded if
    ever used directly.
16. **The Longshore** — coastal, tool-using but pre-metal, preserve
    boundary is a literal coastline, contested every time sea levels
    shift.
17. **Karn-eth** — cave-adapted, bioluminescent fungal symbiosis on
    their own skin, mistaken for Ashen Choir relatives at first contact,
    unrelated.
18. **The Underglass** — live inside naturally-formed silica domes on a
    high-radiation world, preserve status is functionally the only
    reason they're still alive post-contact, COE radiation shielding
    tech quietly extended their survival odds even while restricting
    their development.
19. **Vurn-kesh** — pack-hunting apex predators on their homeworld, the
    one preserve species the COE genuinely fears rather than just
    manages, preserve boundary enforcement is unusually strict here and
    for once actually protective rather than convenient.
20. **The Pale Wick** — bioluminescent moths grown to sapient scale,
    single-generation lifespan measured in COE months, meaning entire
    cultural memory is oral and extremely compressed.
21. **Otheg-vane** — semi-aquatic, egg-laying, matrilineal, closest thing
    to a "trading partner" among the preserve species since their
    culture already valued exchange before contact.
22. **The Cindered** — survivors of their own homeworld's climate
    collapse, pre-spacefaring but post-catastrophe, a preserve species
    whose "protection" arguably came centuries too late for most of what
    they lost.
23. **Krevu-thal** — small, quadrupedal, herd-social, near-identical
    silhouette to Hiopi at a distance, a resemblance that's caused real
    diplomatic friction more than once.
24. **The Last Loom** — weaving/textile culture predates contact by
    millennia, their preserve is the single most-visited by COE
    "cultural tourists," a detail worth using if the exploitation angle
    ever needs a concrete, ugly example rather than an abstract one.
25. **Ossvinth** — deep-cold adapted, low-metabolism, homeworld orbits
    at the outer edge of its system's habitable range, easiest preserve
    to defend simply because almost nothing else wants the real estate.
26. **The Greywake** — post-industrial collapse survivors, not
    pre-industrial, the one preserve species that had spaceflight-
    adjacent tech before losing it to their own civilizational failure,
    a different and more uncomfortable category than "never got there
    yet."

## Note on the naming coincidence bit (items 4, 9, 14)

The three unrelated species sharing "Choir" in their names, and the
Serrat-bound/Carabil aesthetic coincidence, are deliberate running jokes,
not errors, useful texture for a character who's actually studied
xenobiology to grumble about. Feel free to cut any of them if they don't
earn their keep once actually on the page.

## Using this list

Most of these need nothing more than what's here, a name and a line,
existing specifically as scale and as the quiet parallel discussed in
Qiraki_Concept.md. If any one of them becomes plot-relevant (most likely
candidates: the Halewrought for the "protection is actually punitive"
angle, the Last Loom for exploitation-tourism, or Vurn-kesh for the rare
case where preserve status is genuinely protective), give it a proper
character-sheet-style entry at that point rather than trying to flesh
out the whole list preemptively.
