## PART A — WHAT THIS ERA IS, UNDER THE FORMULA

**A position: Mars.** Advancement through conflict. At ordinary scale, the era's mood: a young man getting better at a job. At extreme scale, the override: when the loss is past what one person can hold, the position speaks through him and it sounds like doctrine.

**The chain in one sentence.** A man whose want is that nobody leaves is posted to the one job where everybody does, and every book is what he does about that with no standing to do anything.

**Trav's Matter across the era:** none (Book 8) → low (Books 9 to 13) → mid (Book 14, and only at the very end). Every reaction he has before Book 14 has nowhere to go, and where it goes instead is the era.

**B in, Trav:** a socket he does not understand, a mother's "see you," a friend's question cut off three times, seven years of Reqa's hands, a calibration bond with something that wants to kill things, a pain he has adapted to, and no experience of being responsible for anyone. **The want is present and has never been hit.**
