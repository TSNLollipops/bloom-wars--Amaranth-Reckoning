## PART ONE — THE ENGINE

The author’s rule, on file: each action a reaction, the reaction true to that character and possibly nothing, every character a person, the chain continues, the book writes itself. The formula is that rule made operable. This part states how a forty-two-book plan obeys it.

### 1.1 Stations, not rails

Every locked beat in v1 Part Four is a **station**. The chain must pass through every station. It chooses its own road. Where an era outline below arrives at a station by a different road than v1 planned, the road is the new plan and the station is unchanged. Nothing locked moves.

### 1.2 c comes from E

Every book entry carries an **E→c** field: what this book leaves inside whose B, and what the next book opens on. The next book’s opening catalyst is drawn from that field and not from the ledger. If a station falls at the opening of a book, the road into it is still built from the previous E. A promotion, a purchase or a rotation can be the station a book opens on. It cannot be the reason the book opens.

### 1.3 Trav reacts

Gate 1 is run against his long-term want, **don’t lose anyone else**, every time someone is lost or nearly lost. The lock that he does not process backward governs the thought phase only. His default animal is all-the-way-down forward: instinct moves toward the problem, thought narrows to the mechanical, action is short and immediate. So a want-hit produces an **action**, not a silence. The action is mechanically shaped, usually mis-aimed, has a cost, and propagates into the next book. This is the single largest change from v1 and every era below is built on it.

What this does not change: he never narrates it, never names it, never reflects. The prose gets shorter and more mechanical. The sentences do the work the interiority is forbidden from doing.

### 1.4 D becomes a

Every book designs at least one **collision**, one character’s D landing on another as their a, and every era designs at least one **loop** on the relationship that era is about. The Style Guide’s thesis ban is untouched. A reaction is not a thesis. A woman choosing where to sit is a D.

### 1.5 Idle is the floor

Most paragraphs. Ordinary chapters. Never the load-bearing beat for the character whose want that beat touches.

### 1.6 Time runs both ways

Every era carries at least one arrival that is **too early**: a tool before its job, a plan before its war, a channel before its need.

### 1.7 E lands in a person first

Each book’s forward pressure lives in a named B before it lives in the reader. The unspendable register (PAY Part Four) is the exception and the accidental-defence device is its mechanism: those silences are Gate 7 by design and are the only reader-only channel the series keeps.

### 1.8 A is scoped

Each era carries a dominant position from the formula’s twelve. At ordinary scale it is mood. At extreme scale it overrides the animal’s action, which is the formula’s one proven planet effect. Named so a drafter knows which way the world leans when a beat gets past what a person can hold.

**Reqa Tremblay.** Build the thing in her head before her hands fail it · take it apart / run it forward / work more, faster, worse · Anger, read as speed · Instinct reaches for the object, thought anticipates the failure, action displaces the feeling onto the bench and calls the weld the answer.

**Vrassik.** The city, and then whatever replaces it · toward the body / where do I stand / announce and move · Anger, worn as volume · Instinct closes distance, thought reads the room’s order, action says it first. His splits are rare and he makes a joke inside each one.

**Coherence of Process.** Coherence, held alone · process / consequence at a long horizon / compress · No default echo transfers. **Alina Firemoss.** Know what a thing was for · read the ledger / find the change / pull or leave it · Anger, held as conservation · Instinct conserves, thought reconstructs, action intervenes only on the strength of a record. **Standing note.** The formula never supplies the tiebreaker. History does. The lines above are the history compressed. Where a beat below picks an echo, it cites which of these it is picking from.

## PART FOUR — MATTER, STOPGAP

The Agency Ledger the formula points to is not in the project. Until it is, each book entry in the era outlines carries a one-line **Matter** field for the principals present, scored low / mid / high / full for how much standing they have to convert the book’s central reaction into an effective action. A hard structural zero is scored **none** and named (cannot speak on mission, held at a door, sensor returns only).

The series-length shape, stated once here: **Trav’s Matter climbs from none to full across Eras II to VI and his want does not change, so the same reaction that had no outlet in Book 8 has every outlet in Book 32, and the D is the same.** That is the bunker parallel, produced by gate 2 rather than by anybody drawing it.

## PART FIVE — THE SIX DEVICES, RE-HOUSED

Same six as v1. What changes is the rule that each instance is somebody’s D or somebody’s a.

**D1. The web of weird orders.** Each instance is a c for Trav and a D for whoever issued it. From Era III the issuer is sometimes him, and his own D is the web’s instance. **New under the formula:** the web has a version from below. Book 16, Reqa builds an unlicensed successor to a one-run module that nearly killed him, and files it correctly, and the pegger in Book 20 does not flag it, because it has a form. Same species of act, opposite direction, nobody connects them.

**D2. The accidental defence of the universe.** Unchanged. Gate 7 by design. The one reader-only device. Six instances, none of them anyone’s D on the page, and that is the point.

**D3. The propaganda fragment.** Unchanged in mechanism. Under the formula every fragment is the wider world’s Gate 7, an institution reacting to a consequence it did not witness, which is why the device works and why rebel fragments in Book 39 read identically.

**D4. The bench chapter.** Unchanged in allocation, three per book, two in Era VI. **Re-housed:** every bench chapter is a collision. Somebody’s D (a number, a tolerance, a refusal) lands on somebody else’s bench as their a. Reqa against Alina is the standing pair, curator against builder, and each is proven right once.

**D5. Yssa’s letters.** Unchanged. She is Earth. One line per book, the same question, and she does not escalate because the position does not rotate.

**D6. The socket.** v1 spent it once, in Book 20, on an audit. **[PROPOSED] It is spent twice in Book 20**, once flagged on a form and once **used**, on Zeteii, at her own request, by Trav, the same act his mother performed on his father’s gear and he performed on Zeteii at seventeen. Functional, not symbolic, which is ERA7’s own standard for it. See ERA3 Book 20. The Era VII appearance is dropped: it has done its job.

## PART SIX — THE FIVE THREADS, AS CHAINS

v1 Part Six described five threads that run the whole way. They are kept and restated as chains, each with the collision that turns it and the loop that is its point.

### 6.1 Trav and Reqa

**The equipment is the chain.** She has sensor returns and no audio from Book 8. Every book she watches telemetry she cannot speak into. Her b⁴ displaces onto the bench. **The Divider is not the argument built into an object, as v1 had it. It is her jealousy built into an object**, and the founding insight arrives in Book 8 as a direct reaction to the first mission she could not talk him through.

Collisions: Book 9 she says the wrong thing and it is a bandwidth question. Book 12 the hangar, and the loop is where he sits. Book 25 she hands him a sword that only works if she was right, and he buys the unit off-page, and she finds it on a manifest. Book 25 also, and this is new: **Zeteii tells him the draw number during the gigafish, in Reqa’s words, because Zeteii sat through the whole build.** Reqa hears it on return telemetry. That is the a that makes Book 46’s radio sentence (PAY spend 6) a D from a chain rather than a beat from a ledger.

Loop: Book 12, the hangar. Book 46, the radio.

### 6.2 Trav and Zeteii

**Her want is one object, singularity and freedom, and his want is people staying. They never conflict.** That is why it is the daily one. Collisions: Book 8, she changes the subject, and it works, and she now owns that tool. Book 20, she asks him to delete something and he does it without asking why. Book 33, throttled, and his action is to file a request at a Function desk and be correctly refused. Book 36, she declines the collective and builds a private channel to him that nothing can index, which is the plurality fear made into hardware and is what the clone runs on for a century.

Loop: Book 35, the vow, three passes, the heaviest configuration in the series. **Book 47 is the same words with the loop deliberately not run.** He asks, she goes, no D returns before the fleet arrives. The catastrophe’s mechanism is structural: a loop that ran once, replaced by a one-way collision, with the same request. Nobody says this. The formula does.

### 6.3 Trav and Vrassik

**His want has a station v1 never placed: the adult carrier-scale win, DEC-15’s reroute.** Placed here. Book 9 he enters MeltaDread’s ritual too early, on arrival, with no standing, because he announces and moves, and loses, and it is the first time on the page he comes second in front of witnesses. Book 13 he wins the city, after the siege has buried most of the field including Trahsin, and Jifsook says so, and he knows. That win is what he gives up in Book 34. Book 41 he enters again at ninety, on the hub world, because his sister died trying to give a generation a city and he is the one who gave one up, and he loses, and makes a joke. FCAST’s rule stands: he never learns about the seat.

Loop: Book 34, under a minute, a joke afterward, and Trav says nothing because Vrassik would hate it.

### 6.4 Ilyen Pral

**Her want is to be right about the one thing she wants, and the formula reads that literally.** She applies to MeltaDread in Book 13 because it is his Guild, and would say the slot, and the slot is real. She starts contradicting him in Book 14 as a reaction to nearly dying because of a correct call, not as a role assigned by research. Book 26 she states the bias as a defect report and he hears it correctly and **changes a number in the planning model**, which is how “nothing changes” is done under the formula: something changes and it is a number. **Book 45 she leaves because the want was satisfied in Book 26.** Being right about it was the want. He was never the want. Eighty years later she notices that, and goes, and the letters read like Yssa’s.

### 6.5 The joke-ritual

**v1 said it becomes how he runs a room and he does not know it. Kept, with a chain under it.** Book 12: he uses it on a dying man on the long flight home, because it is the only tool he has, and it does not work, and that is its first recorded failure. Book 30: it works on a broadcast audience of billions, which is the tool arriving too early for the job it will do in Book 39, where it stops working on soldiers. Book 44 and 49: it works on one person. Never narrated, and now every change in it is a reaction to a specific previous use.
