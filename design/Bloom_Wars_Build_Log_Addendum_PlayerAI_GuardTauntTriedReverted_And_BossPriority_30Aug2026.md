## Addendum, 30 Aug 2026: Player AI hardening pass — full re-baseline, Guard Taunt (tried, measured, reverted), boss-priority discount (shipped, honest-neutral)

Maxime, same day as Tier 0's own class-triangle pass: "make ai smarter... post EA. for now, we gotta focus on making our test bot capable enough it doesn't falsify our test, because I keep finding mission much easier than they should because our test ai sucks." Scoped and sequenced in `design/Bloom_Wars_PlayerAI_Hardening_And_Alicialisation_Roadmap_v1.md` (this same session, earlier) — this addendum is Tiers A through (a tried-and-reverted) B/C of that plan.

### Tier A — full 40-mission re-baseline, and a real finding it surfaced

`npm run sim:batch -- 25` (1000 runs) against the post-Tier-0 build, before touching anything else: **72% aggregate (716/1000)**, consistent with Tier 0's own 73.5% figure — the small drift is ordinary batch-to-batch sampling noise (`MEEPS_DODGE_CHANCE`/Bloom on-hit effects both roll real randomness), not a regression from anything landing between the two runs.

**The real finding, not previously written down this plainly**: reading the full per-mission breakdown (not just the aggregate), `commander_down` — not a plain squad-wipe `loss` — is the dominant failure mode campaign-wide, not just Mission 12's own already-documented case. `mission_amaranth_8` went 0/25 with **25/25 runs ending in commander_down**. `mission_amaranth_3` (0/25), `mission_amaranth_5` (3/25), `mission_amaranth_12` (3/25), `mission_amaranth_21` (9/25) all show the same pattern: the squad isn't being ground down, Rourke specifically is dying and ending the attempt instantly (`engine/mission.ts`'s `handleDowned()`) while her escort is often still standing. `player_ai_engine.md`'s own "Missions 3 and 5 confirmed still at ~0%" note undersold this — it's not two missions, it's the single most common way a hard mission fails across the whole campaign. This reframed the rest of the pass: the 28 Aug commander-protection work (`COMMANDER_RETREAT_HP_FRACTION`, `commanderSafePathPrefix`) only ever changed how Rourke moves and retreats *herself* — nothing on the squad's side ever actively pulled fire off her.

### Tier B — Guard Taunt: tried, measured, reverted (a real negative result, not a gap)

abil_taunt (Meeps, Mission-8-unlock) is the shipped, obvious answer: while active, every hostile targeting tier picks the taunting unit first, ahead of even the mech "kill the Munti" priority (`engine/ai.ts`'s `taunting` check). Built `canGuardTaunt(unit)` and `frontLineAllyToProtect(allUnits, enemies, turn)` (`combat.ts`) — a non-protected Meeps with an unspent charge taunts the moment a front-line-protected ally (commander/Munti) is *visible* to any living enemy — and a new `guard_taunt` branch in `index.ts`'s priority chain (above the retreat check, below kill/critical-repair), plus the matching `taunt` action and `Mission.taunt()` dispatch in both `run.ts` and `runBatch.ts`.

**Isolated in its own `sim:batch` run (boss-priority held off), this made things measurably worse, not better**: aggregate **72% → 65%** at n=1000. Missions that were fine at baseline collapsed outright — `mission_amaranth_21` (Wellroot) 36%→0%, `mission_amaranth_25` 100%→20%, `mission_amaranth_26` 100%→28%. Combined with the boss-priority change it was still a net regression (72%→67%), confirming Taunt was the actual cause, not an interaction.

**Root cause, traced rather than assumed**: Taunt's redirect lasts exactly one hostile turn (until the taunting unit's own next turn), but activating it costs that unit's *entire* turn — no move, no attack — and burns the mission's one and only charge. Visibility (seen by any living enemy) turned out to be far too loose a trigger: it fires almost immediately in most fights, buying one turn of insurance at the cost of a full turn of squad damage/positioning, and leaves nothing in reserve for the actual crisis moment later in a longer engagement. Exactly the "a wrong heuristic is worse than the current honest zero" case `index.ts`'s own header already warns about for Ambush/Interdict/Sensor Sweep — same call applied here.

**Reverted, not deleted.** `canGuardTaunt`/`frontLineAllyToProtect` stay defined in `combat.ts` (correct, reusable primitives), and the `taunt` action / `guard_taunt` reason stay in `types.ts` and the dispatch in `run.ts`/`runBatch.ts` — none of it is ever invoked right now (`index.ts`'s guard-taunt call site is commented out with the numbers above in place, not silently dropped), so a future pass with a real crisis-only trigger (HP-threshold-gated *and* visibility-gated, reserved rather than spent on first sight) has the plumbing ready without needing to rebuild it or rediscover this failure mode blind.

### Tier C — boss/priority-target awareness: shipped, honestly flat so far

`player_ai_engine.md`'s own listed gap: "`focus_weak` has no notion of 'this is the boss'... surfaced hard on Mission 21 (Heartwood)... not touched by the 30 Aug class-triangle fix — Bloom carry no `path`." Added `EMERGENT_BOSS_PRIORITY_DISCOUNT = 0.5` and threaded an opt-in `prioritizeBosses` flag through `targetPriorityScore`/`weakestTarget` (`combat.ts`) — an emergent-tier target (`engine/ai.ts`'s own `intelligenceOf`, already the game's real data-driven boss marker, reused rather than inventing a second one) has its raw-toughness score halved before ranking, so it doesn't read as effectively "ignore forever" the way a flat `currentHp * effectiveDefense` formula does against something with 400+ Endurance. Scoped exactly the way Tier 0 scoped its own class-triangle weighting: **only** `focusFireTargetInRange`'s in-range call passes `true` — the two distant chase-target `weakestTarget(enemies)` calls in `index.ts` (`advance_into_range`, `seek_fight`) are untouched, for the identical reason Tier 0's own addendum already gives for those two sites (no distance/exposure term exists yet to weigh against a longer, riskier approach).

**Honest result**: flat, not a win — n=1000 aggregate 71% vs. 72% baseline; a dedicated n=100 run on `mission_amaranth_21` specifically (the one live emergent-boss mission in the current campaign, `bloom_wellroot`) came back 26% vs. 28% baseline, also flat. That mission's own failure mode is still overwhelmingly `commander_down` (72-74/100 either way) — the same campaign-wide problem Tier B tried and failed to fix — which swamps whatever this nudge contributes. Shipped anyway because it's a correct, zero-regression fix for a real documented gap, not because it's proven itself yet; worth re-measuring once commander-exposure protection actually gets solved for real and stops masking the signal.
