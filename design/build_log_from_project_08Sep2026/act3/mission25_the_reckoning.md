# Mission 25 — The Reckoning

**Objective:** eliminate_all. **Map:** widest single-turn frontage in the campaign, 34×16, 5 spawn points — built deliberately so a big wave reads as a tide, not a queue. **Enemy:** retuned twice for two different squad sizes.

Meridian's Oath (Fire Support re-fluffed as Providence's own guns, repositioned inland — a creative call, not confirmed lore, easy to rename if it clashes with anything) debuts here.

**Tuning, 8-pilot squad:** first draft (12 Crawlmass + 6 Splitfang + 4 Sporethrower, 22 total, all turn 1) was 0/15 — too much arriving with zero stagger for any squad to out-attrit. Cut to 6/3/2 (11 total): 14/15 (~93%).

**Retuned again for the 12-pilot Act III squad** once the Third Lance came online: 18 total felt trivial, 25 introduced a real cliff (100% at 23, down to ~73% with real losses at 25 — not a gradient), 21 undershot again. Locked at 23 (13/6/4): 100% wins, long fights. Not chased further past that — a real cliff in the data isn't worth sanding down to one enemy's precision.
