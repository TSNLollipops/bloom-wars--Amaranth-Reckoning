# Mission 33 — The Innermost Ring

**Objective:** hold_zone (holdUntilTurn 16 / turnLimit 22). **Map:** single-doorway walled room, same proven shape as Missions 27/29/32 rather than risking a first-time two-doorway room on the campaign's last two hold_zone missions — "the ring" reads through wave count and staggered approach corridors instead. **Enemy:** 5 waves.

First-draft counts (12/6/5/8/5) with `holdUntilTurn 12` went 20/20 win at exactly turn 12 every time — wave 5 (spawned turn 13) never got a chance to matter. Pushed counts to 14/7/6/10/6 and `holdUntilTurn` to 16 so all five waves are actually live.

**Result:** 16/20 (80%), real variance in the failure mode (some losses from a hostile slipping onto the hold zone uncontested, some from the turn limit expiring first).
