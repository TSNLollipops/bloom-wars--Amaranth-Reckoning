# Mission 16 — Collaborators

**Objective:** eliminate_all (turn 12). **Map:** House Amaranth depot, 24×14. **Enemy:** 5 House Amaranth Conscripts (4 distinct archetypes, 2+1+1+1). **Bonus:** rescue_pilot, reused as-is but reframed in fiction as a surrendering conscript — a deliberate reuse, not the only shape this could have taken (a dedicated surrender/dialogue mechanic was the real alternative), flagged as such rather than presented as obviously correct.

Tuning was the swingiest search in the batch: 8 total conscripts (2 each of 4 archetypes) was 0/8; Mission 6's own 4-total/1-each precedent came back too easy at 8/8; 6 total landed near 1/8. Settled on 5 total (2+1+1+1).

**Result:** ~63% (10/16), kept deliberately swingy rather than smoothed to match the rest of the batch — "these aren't Bloom, they fight like we do" (Anand's own briefing line) is the mission's whole point; a human-piloted enemy reading as harder and less consistent than a swarm archetype was judged as earning its own difficulty character. Flagged as a real choice, open to Maxime's read.
