# Mission 6 — House Colors

**Objective:** eliminate_all. **Map:** border checkpoint gate, 20×12. **Enemy:** 4 House Amaranth Line Troopers.

First mission to field named, game-acknowledged hostile mechs (`AMARANTH_HOSTILE_MECHS`) — the deliberate opposite of Mission 1a's "Unmarked Mech" quiet-critique discipline, which is specifically about that one site's own defenders, not a blanket rule. Required a merged hostile-mech lookup table (`ALL_HOSTILE_MECHS`) rather than editing the original table in place, same anti-duplication call as `pilotRegistry.ts`.

Carries an early dialogue event flagging Col. Marrow's first distant sighting — the setup for her eventual real engagement at Mission 20 and closing arc at Mission 28.
