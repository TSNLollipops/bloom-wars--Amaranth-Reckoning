# Build Log Addendum — Mission 16 rescue-pickup fix + Mission 17 finding (30 Aug 2026)

Two related reports from Maxime, same underlying cause. First, "mission 16 only give us 5 enemy mech, we field 10 of them. how does our bot lose lole?" — then, once that was traced, "mission 17 can def have the double number of bloom it spawned with," which turned out to hit the exact same wall from a different direction.

**Important clarification first: "our bot" here is the sim/test player AI** (`src/sim/playerAi/`, run via `npm run sim` / `npm run sim:batch`), the tool this whole session has used to verify mission balance — not anything in the shipped game. Maxime plays every real mission himself; nothing here changes what he experiences directly. It matters anyway, because this bot's win-rate numbers are exactly what every Tier 6 spawn-variety edit this session has been judged against — a real weakness in the bot's own decision-making means some of those judgments were resting on shakier ground than the percentages alone suggested.

## Mission 16 — the actual mechanism, and a real fix

Ran the sim batch first: `WIN=43/60 (72%) COMMANDER_DOWN=17` — not LOSS, not TIMEOUT. All 17 losses were Rourke (the commander) dying, which ends the mission attempt outright regardless of the other 9 units. A verbose trace made the mechanism obvious: Rourke happened to be the unit standing next to Mission 16's rescue NPC on turn 2, so she's the one who picked it up. From that point on, `carryingRescueId` takes over her entire turn — combat is engine-refused while carrying, and the AI's own carry logic just beelines for the exit with zero retreat or caution logic. By turn 5 she'd reached the far exit corner completely alone, several turns ahead of the other 9 units, and got focus-fired down before anyone caught up. The 10-vs-5 numeric advantage was never the issue — it's that the single unit whose death ends everything got sent in with no ability to defend herself.

This is exactly the same class of gap this codebase's own "commander protection" system (28 Aug 2026) already exists to close for ordinary combat decisions — it was just never extended to the rescue-pickup branch. **Fix:** a front-line-protected unit (the commander, or the squad's Munti — same pairing the existing system already uses) now simply never volunteers to pick up a rescue NPC, and never wanders toward one either. If nobody else ever reaches it, the ~45-point bonus goes unclaimed — a trivially better outcome than the whole mission ending. Shipped in `src/sim/playerAi/index.ts`, typecheck/lint/test clean (1140/1140).

## The regression that fix exposed — and Mission 17's own finding

Re-running Mission 16 after the fix landed: `WIN=26/60 (43%)`, WORSE than before, still all via COMMANDER_DOWN. Tracing it: with the rescue-carry "distraction" removed, Rourke's own baseline combat AI took over — and it has an independent, already-documented weakness (the commander-protection code's own comments call it out directly: "she got exposed two ways — scouting ahead alone on her own higher Meeps move range... a cohesion gap"). The existing safeguard (`commanderSafePathPrefix`, which caps her from advancing past her own most-forward living ally) doesn't fully hold when SHE is often that most-forward unit herself, simply by being the fastest class on the roster. The old rescue-carry bug was, by accident, "protecting" her from her own AI's other weakness more often than it was killing her outright — removing the accident made the underlying gap visible, not worse in itself.

Maxime's very next message — "mission 17 can def have the double number of bloom it spawned with" — ran straight into the same wall from a completely different angle. Mission 17 (The Wellroot Uncovered) baselines at a healthy-looking 88-92% win rate. Doubling its three enemy waves (Crawlmass 6→12, Splitfang 1→2, Sporethrower 1→2) was tried and sim-tested (n=60) exactly like every other Tier 6 edit this session — and collapsed hard. So did each archetype tested ALONE:

- Crawlmass alone, 6→12: 73% win (COMMANDER_DOWN 3→16)
- Splitfang alone, 1→2: 73% win (LOSS — Solheim, the extract target — 3→10)
- Sporethrower alone, 1→2: 67% win (COMMANDER_DOWN 3→19)

Every single increment, even the smallest one tried, roughly quintupled the commander-death rate. The mission's high baseline percentage was hiding almost no real margin — because the actual bottleneck isn't overall squad strength, it's the same commander-exposure mechanism Mission 16 just surfaced, and this mission's `extract_unit` objective (a long march, `turnLimit: 16`) gives that mechanism many more turns to find an opening than a shorter mission would. **Not shipped** — Mission 17 stays at its original counts, with a comment documenting exactly what was tried and why it was reverted, same discipline as Missions 5 and 10 in the first Tier 6 batch.

## What this means going forward

The sim bot's commander-protection system does real work and isn't broken in the sense of doing nothing — but it has a genuine, now twice-confirmed gap: it doesn't reliably keep the commander safe once enemy density or her own speed puts her ahead of a "front line" that's really just wherever she already is. This isn't a data-balance problem any individual mission's spawn list can fix, and it isn't the kind of unilateral call to make without the same full-campaign sim-batch verification Tier 0's own class-triangle fix used (a 400-run regression check across every mission) — a proper fix here has real reach across every mission that isn't `extract_unit` (the one objective type this protection is deliberately switched off for already). Flagging this as a real next task rather than attempting a same-day rewrite of campaign-wide AI logic off one or two missions' worth of evidence.

**Net of today's two related passes:** Mission 16's rescue-pickup bug is a genuine fix, shipped. Mission 17's requested doubling is NOT shipped, and Mission 16 itself likely still has a real win-rate gap worth another look once the deeper cohesion issue is addressed — the rescue-pickup fix alone doesn't get it back to a healthy number by itself.
