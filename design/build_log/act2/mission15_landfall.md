# Mission 15 — Landfall

**Objective:** contested_landing (turn 14) — the objective type's debut. **Map:** beach assault, 24×14. **Enemy:** 10 Crawlmass + 6 Splitfang (turn 1) + 4 Crawlmass (turn 3).

Confirmed via `AskUserQuestion` before building: "deploy under fire" shape — hostiles already positioned at/near the deploy zone at turn 1, no grace period, mechanically `eliminate_all`-shaped underneath.

**Result:** ~93% across the sample, deliberately not pushed to a clean 100% — real losses and some genuinely long grinds even among the wins are a deliberate design choice, matching what "no grace period, hostiles already at your throat at turn 1" should feel like. Flagged explicitly as a call made this session, open to revisiting if it should come down further.
