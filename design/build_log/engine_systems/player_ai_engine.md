# The Player AI Engine (`src/sim/playerAi/`)

Maxime: "make it a separate engine we can plug into our future games... something we can reuse like the characterisation formula." Replaced the old flat `src/sim/testPlayerAi.ts`. Structure: `types.ts` (shared shapes), `combat.ts` (kill/focus-fire/retreat/kiting/cohesion/terrain), `support.ts` (repair decisions), `index.ts` (priority chain: kill > critical repair > retreat/hold > routine repair > focus fire > advance > seek fight, plus objective branches — see below).

## What it does today

- **Combat basics** — kill-if-lethal, focus fire on a squad-shared weakest target (not per-attacker), retreat/kiting at low HP, repair (heal-in-place, two HP thresholds).
- **Class-triangle-aware focus fire** (30 Aug 2026, Consolidated Build Plan Tier 0 — "give the bot real class-triangle-aware target selection"). `weakestTarget`'s ranking now optionally weighs a target's raw toughness against `data/combatTables.ts`'s existing POWER matrix (the same numbers `resolveMechAttack` already fights by) via `combat.ts`'s `targetPriorityScore`/`squadAveragePowerAgainst` — reuses the already-approved balance table rather than inventing a new one. **Scoped narrowly, on real evidence, not the whole file:** only `focusFireTargetInRange` (the squad's shared choice among targets already in range this turn) passes an allies list; the two `weakestTarget(enemies)` calls in `index.ts` that pick which distant enemy to walk toward (`advance_into_range`, `seek_fight`) are deliberately left unweighted. A first pass weighted all three call sites and a 400-run batch (all 40 missions, `npm run sim`-equivalent harness) came back 67% aggregate win vs. a 73.25% baseline — a real regression: chasing a distant type-advantaged target with zero distance/exposure awareness pulled the squad toward farther, riskier approaches purely for the matchup. Narrowing to focus-fire only landed 73.5%, flat against baseline, while still measurably improving the mech-duel missions this item was meant to fix (Mission 6 "House Colors": 82% → 92% at n=50). See `index.ts`'s own comments at both `weakestTarget(enemies)` call sites before re-weighting them — that exact change was tried and measured worse.
- **Boss/priority-target awareness** (30 Aug 2026, Player AI hardening pass — `Bloom_Wars_Build_Log_Addendum_PlayerAI_GuardTauntTriedReverted_And_BossPriority_30Aug2026.md`). Same `targetPriorityScore`/`weakestTarget` machinery, a second opt-in flag: an emergent-tier target (`engine/ai.ts`'s `intelligenceOf`) has its raw-toughness score halved (`EMERGENT_BOSS_PRIORITY_DISCOUNT`) before ranking, so a 400+-Endurance boss like Wellroot doesn't read as "ignore forever." Same narrow scope as the class-triangle fix — only `focusFireTargetInRange` passes it. Honest result: flat, not a proven win — n=1000 aggregate 71% vs. 72% baseline, and a dedicated n=100 run on `mission_amaranth_21` (the one live emergent-boss mission) came back 26% vs. 28%, also flat. Shipped anyway (zero regression, closes a real documented gap below) but not yet delivering — that mission's real failure mode is the commander_down item below, which likely swamps this signal.
- **Squad cohesion** — `cohesiveMoveToward` caps how far a unit advances ahead of its nearest living ally; a wounded, unspotted unit with nothing to kill/heal regroups toward the squad instead of chasing alone. Fixed a real oscillation bug (retreat/seek_fight ping-ponging) and a real infinite-regroup stall along the way.
- **Terrain/cover** — reachable-tile scoring folds in `defenceStars` as a tiebreaker (tuned down 5-10x from its first cut, which was strong enough to fight the cohesion fix).
- **Objective awareness** — reads a narrow `PlayerAiMissionContext` (objective, extractUnitId, bonusObjective kind, holdZone, exitTiles) and branches: beeline for an exit while carrying a rescue; pick up an adjacent uncarried rescuable NPC; clear bloom for a Munti on a clear-bloom objective/bonus (with a first `use_screen` heuristic layered on top, though it turned out to rarely actually fire in practice — see the Screen note below); path to the exit for the named `extract_unit` target; converge on the nearest hold-zone tile for `hold_zone`; escort convergence on the exit once nothing better is left to do (`escort_to_exit`, fixes a real deadlock where idle escorts froze and boxed in the extract target).
- **Civilian awareness** — none; civilians run their own separate `decideCivilianAction` (see `civilian_extraction_system.md`), not this engine.

## Known, un-fixed limitations (flagged repeatedly, not silently)

- **Commander protection only ever covers her own movement, not the squad's behavior around her — confirmed the single biggest lever left (30 Aug, full 40-mission re-baseline), and confirmed AGAIN after Taunt went reusable.** `commander_down` (not a plain squad-wipe loss) is the dominant failure mode campaign-wide, not just Mission 12's already-documented case: `mission_amaranth_8` hit 25/25 commander_down at n=25; 3/5/12/21 all show the same shape. Guard Taunt (a non-protected Meeps taunts to pull fire off a front-line ally) has now been tried **three times**, reverted all three: (1) visibility-triggered against the OLD once-per-mission Taunt — 72%→65% aggregate, the mission's one charge burned too early. Then Taunt itself was redesigned reusable (see below), specifically to unblock a retry — and it still didn't land clean: (2) the same visibility-only trigger against the now-reusable ability fixed `mission_amaranth_8` outright (0%→84%) but drove `mission_amaranth_21` to 0% (25/25 commander_down, worse than baseline) — a Meeps with no defensive bonus taunting every visible turn just dies to the swarm it's drawing. (3) an HP-gated variant (only guard an ally already below 60% hp, not merely seen) fixed `mission_amaranth_21`/`12` but broke `mission_amaranth_8` right back to 0% (fires too late for a mission where the fatal hit lands the same turn the commander is first seen) and still left `mission_amaranth_26` regressed. Three attempts, three different failure shapes, no clean win yet. `canGuardTaunt`/`frontLineAllyToProtect` (`combat.ts`) stay defined, unused, HP-gate baked in from attempt 3 as the closer-to-neutral default — the real fix needs a trigger that reasons about the taunting unit's own survival odds (threat count / incoming lethality), not just ally visibility or ally hp. Full numbers: `Bloom_Wars_Build_Log_Addendum_PlayerAI_GuardTauntTriedReverted_And_BossPriority_30Aug2026.md` (attempt 1) and `Bloom_Wars_Build_Log_Addendum_TauntNoCharge_GuardTauntRetried_30Aug2026.md` (attempts 2-3, plus Taunt's own no-charge/root-lock redesign).
- **No ability usage, Taunt included** (see above for why it's not simply "unused" but "tried three times, actively harmful in every attempt so far"). Ambush/Interdict/Screen/Sensor Sweep are all real, shipped verbs the bot still never uses (Screen got one narrow, rarely-firing heuristic — see Mission 3's file). A wrong heuristic for an ability is worse than an honest zero — now demonstrated three separate times with real numbers, not just asserted. Note: Taunt itself is no longer charge-limited in the real game (30 Aug redesign, Maxime: "make taunt like ambush... no charge, just plain use" — matches Ambush's own shape exactly, reusable posture, whole-turn cost only) and now also roots whatever it redirects (30 Aug, PvP-facing: "taunt should also lock the target in place so they dont run away") — neither change affects this section's own finding, since the sim AI's Guard Taunt heuristic is what's reverted, not the ability.
- **`focus_weak` has no notion of "this is the boss" — partially addressed 30 Aug, not yet proven.** A boss-priority discount now exists (see the "What it does today" entry above) but measured flat (not improved) on the one live emergent-boss mission at n=100 — that mission's real failure mode is the commander_down item above, which likely swamps this signal. Worth re-measuring once commander protection actually improves.
- **No real detour-seeking.** `cohesiveMoveToward`'s distance-field search is terrain-only and greedy — several idle units standing shoulder-to-shoulder can still wall off a narrow approach even with occupied-exit-tile awareness (Mission 23; recurred at squad-scale on Mission 26).
- **The sim is not a difficulty oracle.** Missions 3 and 5 — the **archived, non-shipping Team One slice**, not the live Amaranth campaign — went 0% under the bot and were cleared easily by Maxime playing for real. Confirmed still true post-Tier-0. Lower-priority than it first looked once the 30 Aug re-baseline showed the live Amaranth campaign has the same class of problem (commander_down), worse, and campaign-wide — see the first entry above.
- **No distance/exposure awareness in target selection, even now.** The 30 Aug fix only weighs type advantage where the target is already in range (no extra travel/risk to get there). The bot still doesn't reason about "is closer/safer worth more than a better matchup" for anything it has to walk to — see this file's own note above on why that specific version was tried and reverted, not just never attempted.
